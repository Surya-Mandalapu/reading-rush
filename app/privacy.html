Privacy, in plain language.

Last updated October 15, 2026

Your study progress

Answers, session history, marked questions, and highlights are stored in this browser. They are not sent to Google Analytics. Guest and signed-in account progress are stored separately on this device. Signing in does not sync progress to another device. Export a backup from the practice app before clearing your browser data.

Account sign-in

DosPrep uses Firebase Authentication for Google or email/password sign-in. Firebase processes account information such as your email address and authentication credentials. The app does not send your password to GitHub or include it in analytics. Signing out leaves your account's study history saved on this device, separate from guest progress.

Optional analytics

DosPrep asks before loading Google Analytics. If you allow it, Analytics receives page views and basic feature events, such as starting a round or opening insights. It may process device information, cookies, online identifiers, and approximate location. DosPrep does not include account emails, typed answers, question text, highlights, or account user IDs in these events. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.

You can change your preference below. Declining does not affect practice. Existing Analytics cookies may remain in your browser until you delete them.

External services

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process request information such as IP addresses. Video links open external sites that follow their own privacy policies. See Google's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement.

Removing data

Clear this site's browser storage to remove locally saved study progress and preferences. Clearing browser storage does not delete a Firebase account. Account deletion support is not available in the app yet.
