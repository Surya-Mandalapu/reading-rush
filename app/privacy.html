Privacy, in plain language.

Last updated October 23, 2026

Your study progress

Guest answers, session history, marked questions, and highlights stay in this browser. For signed-in accounts, these records also sync privately to Cloud Firestore in the United States, linked to your Firebase account ID. Your own account can read and update its progress; other users cannot. Answers save after each submission, including partial rounds and retries. Local copies and pending changes remain on this device for offline use and upload after you return online while signed in. Wait for “Progress synced” before clearing browser data. Progress is never sent to Google Analytics. Guest progress is uploaded only if you choose to bring it into your signed-in account.

Account sign-in

DosPrep uses Firebase Authentication for Google or email/password sign-in. Firebase processes account information such as your email address and authentication credentials. The app does not send your password to GitHub or include it in analytics. Signing out leaves your account's local backup and unsent changes on this device, separate from guest progress. Sign back into that account to resume syncing. Other accounts do not receive its records.

Live Quiz rooms

Live Quiz uses Firebase Authentication, including anonymous guest identities, and Cloud Firestore in the United States. Room codes, nicknames, membership, and submitted answers are stored online so the room can play together. Other players and the host can see your nickname and whether you submitted. Your choice stays private until everyone answers or the host reveals early; it is then visible to everyone in the room. Room access expires after about 23 hours. The host can delete a finished room and its answers; expiration restricts access but does not automatically erase stored documents. Live Quiz answers are not added to your solo study history or sent to analytics. Anonymous room sign-in does not move your solo guest history into a signed-in account.

Automatic question recording and community timings

Every new submitted answer in solo practice and Live Quiz contributes automatically to the site-wide answer count, including retries and repeat questions. This is part of the practice feature and has no opt-in switch. Historical answers are not backfilled. Firebase keeps private duplicate-check markers containing an opaque account or guest ID, question ID, mode, eligibility, and submission date. These markers contain no answer duration, correctness, answer text, name, or email. Event identifiers are hashed before upload.

Community timing records contain only question-level sample counts, total seconds, and histograms, split into All, Correct, and Incorrect groups. They contain no account IDs, event IDs, or per-person timing rows. Solo and Live Quiz have separate aggregates. Active timing starts after the question images are ready, excludes pauses and hidden-tab time, and stops at submission; Live Quiz upload time is excluded. Timings from 1 second to 2 hours enter benchmarks; other submissions still count. Retries are timed independently. The site shows comparisons after 20 samples in the selected group; samples are attempts, not distinct people.

Your private signed-in history still includes your own answer times so you can review your progress; it is separate from community statistics. Firebase authentication and private duplicate-check markers protect writes and prevent replayed uploads from counting twice. This does not promise that all service metadata or aggregate changes are impossible to correlate. Unsent timing values temporarily remain in this device’s offline queue, capped at 100 submissions and retained for up to 24 hours. Old individual duration and correctness fields in community duplicate-check records have been removed; aggregate totals are retained.

Optional analytics

DosPrep asks before loading Google Analytics. If you allow it, Analytics receives page views and basic feature events, such as starting a round or opening insights. It may process device information, cookies, online identifiers, and approximate location. DosPrep does not include account emails, typed answers, question text, highlights, or account user IDs in these events. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.

You can change your preference below. Declining does not affect practice. Existing Analytics cookies may remain in your browser until you delete them.

Project Pulse

If you allow analytics, DosPrep also sends page views to Project Pulse, a private dashboard for the site owner. It uses a random browser identifier stored locally for up to 90 days, the page path without query strings, referring domain, and visit time. Project Pulse does not store IP addresses, account details, or practice answers. It respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Visit records are retained for 90 days. Change your analytics preference above to stop future events; clear browser storage to remove your identifier.

External services

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process request information such as IP addresses. Video links open external sites that follow their own privacy policies. See Google's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement.

Removing data

Clear this site's browser storage to remove local study progress, pending changes, and preferences. This does not remove synced Firestore progress or your Firebase account; signing back in downloads your synced history. Restoring a backup replaces the signed-in account's progress on all devices and records removal markers for excluded records. Account and cloud-data deletion controls are not available in the app yet. Export a backup before clearing unsynced data.
