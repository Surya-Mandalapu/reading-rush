A few good questions

Before you begin.

Can I practice without an account?

Yes. Guest practice is available. Your history, highlights, and marked questions stay in your browser. You can export a backup from the practice app.

Does signing in sync my progress?

Yes. Sign in with the same account to sync your practice history, marked questions, and highlights across devices. Changes are also kept on this device when offline. Guest progress stays separate unless you choose to bring it into your account.

Are the videos explanations of each exact question?

The videos teach the question type. Questions that share a skill reuse the same video lessons. The original written rationale explains the individual question.

Is DosPrep affiliated with College Board?

No. DosPrep is an independent study tool. SAT is a trademark of College Board. The practice library comes from the supplied question-bank exports; the app is not an official SAT testing service.
